IMPERIAL Core

A digital corporation architecture of Universal Nano Core Agents (NCA), founded and developed by one human — Alexander Romaskevich (RomaskevicH) — with LLM assistance. The design goal spans one to one billion universal NCA identities, with dynamically assigned professions, HANTER, VELION, CONSTRUCTOR AI and verified governance. The billion-agent target is not an existing deployment.
