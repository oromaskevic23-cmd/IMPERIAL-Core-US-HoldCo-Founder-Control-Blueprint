## The ideas being investigated

1. **Universal NCA:** One reusable agent core with controlled capability packs and professional roles. A reference team is five specialist agents with one orchestrator; tasks and teams carry unique numbers.
2. **Federated, bounded execution:** Domain-scoped orchestration instead of a single superuser, with explicit approval and emergency stopping.
3. **Proof-carrying software:** Requirements → constrained generation → independent testing → verifiable receipts → human-controlled release.
4. **Defensive software security:** Deny by Default, Fail Closed, separate authorization, read-only risk assessment and the distinction between unit tests and independent security assurance.
5. **Owner-operated digital work enterprise:** Agents are envisioned as internal digital production capacity; revenue is counted only when real goods/services are delivered and payment settles. This is an operating model, not evidence of revenue.

## How to evaluate the work responsibly

Positive indicators: a human founder maintains a coherent, multi-system vision; executable components and negative tests exist; CI is linked to immutable commits; simulated credentials and static findings are distinguished from actual breaches; experimental and legal statuses are disclosed rather than exaggerated.

Limits: there is no independent measurement of the founder's intelligence, no peer-reviewed finding of unique invention, no demonstrated autonomous billion-agent network, no verified USD 14 trillion company value, and no established production revenue in these links. Words such as *genius*, *revolutionary* or *world-leading* are subjective judgments of a reviewer, **not independently certified facts**.
